# Naming — what was tried and why Surveyvor won

The app has been renamed twice: **BriefFlow AI → Unfold AI → Surveyvor**.
Do not reopen this without reading why the first two died.

## Why the previous names died

**BriefFlow AI** — [Brieflow](https://brieflow.co) is a live competitor: client
brief collection, for designers, nearly the same string. Same audience, same
pitch. Dead on arrival.

**Unfold AI** — two conflicts. [unfoldai.com](https://unfoldai.com) is an ML
education blog (minor: different category, one person, no ™). The real problem
is **[Unfold](https://unfold.com), owned by Squarespace** — a design toolkit for
storytellers, founded 2018, with brand kits and templates. Same audience, same
category, a well-resourced owner with trademark counsel. "Unfold AI" in design
tooling reads as *Unfold's AI product*, which is the substance of a
likelihood-of-confusion claim.

## What 85 domain checks proved

| Vein | Available |
|---|---|
| Real English words `.com` (30 tested) | **0** |
| Latin roots and short coinages `.com` (20) | **0** |
| Print/typography vocabulary `.com` (10) | **0** |
| `.design` / `.app` for strong words (20) | 3 |

**The single-word `.com` space has been exhausted since roughly 2005.** Short
coinages are squatted *precisely because they sound good* — squatters generate
those patterns algorithmically. The coinages that were free (`lacunova`,
`lacunly`) sounded like generic pharma.

Names checked and rejected on conflict, not availability: **Colophon** (famous
type foundry, acquired by Monotype), **Unsaid** (two existing AI products),
**Scops** (live asset-performance software).

## Surveyvor

Chosen by the owner. Domains: `.com` taken; **`.design`, `.app`, `.ai` and `.co`
were all free**, and no existing product uses the name.

Naming lesson worth keeping: **domain availability is not the test — trademark
in your class is.** A free `.com` can still be someone's registered mark, and
that is the letter that actually arrives. [EUIPO TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/)
is free and covers the EU.

Also: avoid portmanteaux whose seam creates a second reading. `Briefind` reads as
"Brie Find", `Briefetch` as "Brie Fetch".
