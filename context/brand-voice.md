# Brand and copy

The name is only earned if the language follows it. See CLAUDE.md § Language for
the rules that bind code; this file is the source material.

## The governing idea

> **Survey the project before you build it.**

A surveyor does not replace the building plan — they survey the site so the plan
can be trusted. The brief is the plan. Surveyvor is the survey that makes it
trustworthy.

## Live copy

- **Hero:** Survey the project before you build it
- **Sub:** Surveyvor uncovers assumptions, contradictions, missing information
  and unresolved decisions — before they become expensive problems.
- **CTA:** Survey your project →
- **First run:** Give it the mess / It surveys the terrain / Start on solid ground

## Lines held in reserve

Strong, not yet used anywhere — the landing page has no Problem or Why section yet.

**Anti-ChatGPT positioning** (commercially the most important — it answers "why
wouldn't I just paste this into Claude?"):

> Claude can write your brief. Surveyvor can interrogate it.
> AI is very good at filling gaps. Surveyvor is built to show them.
> It doesn't just improve your brief. It questions it.

**Problem section:**

> Most briefs look clearer than they are.

**Alternate heroes, in the owner's preference order:**

1. Survey the project before you build it *(in use)*
2. You said what you want. Surveyvor finds what you missed.
3. Know what you know. See what you don't.
4. The AI that asks what your brief forgot.
5. Don't build on assumptions.

**Campaign lines:** Survey first. Create second. / Clarity before creativity. /
See what the brief can't. / Before this gets expensive.
